Who we are

Faith that becomes action.

We believe compassionate service can open doors to dignity, opportunity, healthy communities and hope.

We are committed to transforming lives in rural Odisha by supporting underprivileged children with education, food, clothing and spiritual care. We empower widows and women through skill development, provide clean drinking water through borewell projects and work with wider community initiatives for rural development.
